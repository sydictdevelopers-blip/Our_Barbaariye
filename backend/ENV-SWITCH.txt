============================================
  Sida loo beddelo: Local ↔ 192 (Webmin)
============================================

HADDA – isticmaal LOCAL (PC-kaaga):
  1. Copy .env.local → .env
  2. Backend dib u bilow (npm start)
  (Haddii aad PostgreSQL local ku sitaan, DB_HOST=localhost wuu shaqeeyaa.)

MARKAAD DIB U NOQOTO 192 (Webmin):
  1. Copy .env.remote-192 → .env
  2. Backend dib u bilow (npm start)
  (192 waa in uu remote u ogolaado – raac FURAN-TALAABO-TALAABO.md.)

Faylka .env ee hadda la isticmaalayaa waa kan backend-ku akhriyo.
